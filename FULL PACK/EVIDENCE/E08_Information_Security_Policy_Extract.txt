NORTHSTAR SKILLS ACADEMY — INFORMATION SECURITY STANDARD (EXTRACT)
Document: NS-SEC-04 | Version 2.1 | Effective: 1 April 2026

1. Service credentials must be stored in the approved secrets vault. Credentials must not be placed in support tickets, screenshots, email, source code, or shared documents.
2. Third-party integrations must receive only the permissions required for their business function.
3. Production service credentials must have an expiry or documented rotation schedule. Exceptions require approval by the IT Administrator.
4. Vendor integrations should be restricted to documented source networks where technically supported.
5. Bulk exports of learner personal information require an approved business request and must be logged.
6. Unusual access volumes or access from a new source network should be reviewed by IT Operations.
7. Accounts belonging to former staff must be disabled on or before the employee's last working day.

This extract is fictional and applies only to the Northstar exercise environment.
